## How it moves

The UVs are split into horizontal bands. Each band gets a short random offset, so the glitch arrives in bursts instead of constant noise. Scanlines come from tiled sine waves; a slower pulse drives the emissive glow.

![Material instance controls for timing, scanlines, and glow](/assets/blog/ue5-terminal-shader/instance-parameters.webp)

The material instance exposes timing, density, and intensity. `GlitchForce` also lets a gameplay event trigger a burst directly.

## Inside the material

I kept distortion, noise, logo sampling, and scanlines in separate graph sections before combining them for Emissive and Opacity Mask.

![Material graph overview](/assets/blog/ue5-terminal-shader/material-graph-overview.webp)

![Final composite nodes](/assets/blog/ue5-terminal-shader/material-composite-nodes.webp)

It's still one unlit masked material—easy to recolor or retime without making another flipbook.
