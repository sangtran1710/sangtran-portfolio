I took inspiration from this [Biohazard Warning VJ Loop](https://pin.it/3Uew9ebLj) and rebuilt it inside Unreal Engine as a runtime procedural shader instead of relying on a video clip or flipbook texture.

The result is a single unlit masked material with zero texture streaming cost, combining horizontal glitch slices, CRT scanlines, and an organic emissive breathing pulse.

## Breakdown & Material Controls

To make the glitch feel mechanical rather than uniform static noise, UV coordinates are quantized into horizontal bands with step divisions and randomized jitter offsets. 

All core behaviors are exposed directly on the material instance (`MI_Biohazard_Terminal`), letting artists tune timing, scanline density, and glitch intensity on the fly:

![Material Instance Details panel showing exposed parameter groups](/assets/blog/ue5-terminal-shader/instance-parameters.webp)

- **Glitch timing**: `BurstInterval`, `GlitchDuration`, and `BurstChance` control random glitch spikes, with `GlitchForce` available as a direct override for scripted gameplay events.
- **Scanlines & Breath**: High-frequency UV sine tiling for CRT phosphor lines, combined with a slow sine pulse scaling emissive intensity.

---

## Graph Organization (172 nodes, 27 parameters)

Because procedural math networks can get messy quickly, the master material (`M_Biohazard_Terminal_Clean`) is divided into dedicated functional blocks:

![Overview of the modular color-coded material graph in Unreal Engine 5](/assets/blog/ue5-terminal-shader/material-graph-overview.webp)

UV slice distortion, jitter noise, logo sampling, and scanlines are calculated in separate passes before feeding into the final composite block to drive Emissive and Opacity Mask.

![Detailed view of the Composite stage combining emissive layers and opacity masks](/assets/blog/ue5-terminal-shader/material-composite-nodes.webp)

Building it procedurally keeps the memory footprint at zero, maintains sharp sub-pixel detail at any camera distance, and allows instant color and timing variations directly in engine.
