The interesting part of a big simulation is often what survives the trip into a game. These are a few Houdini setups I used to turn offline work into fracture pieces, textures, and emitter data.

  <strong className="text-zinc-200 font-medium">Production note:</strong> Offline workflows and isolated real-time tests only. No proprietary engine code or confidential game assets shown.

## Staged collapse

<VideoPlayer
  src="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/wolverine-houdini-rbd-bridge.mp4"
  poster="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/poster-rbd-bridge.webp"
  caption="Bridge collapse test in Houdini."
/>

A bridge reads better when its structure fails in stages. I used a constraint hierarchy for the main frame, a directional forcefield to guide the break, and a separate pass for smaller debris.

## Repeatable prop fractures

<VideoPlayer
  src="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/wolverine-houdini-prop-destruction.mp4"
  previewSrc="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/prop-preview.mp4"

For repeated industrial props, the setup separates inside faces from the outer shell and groups tiny shards into larger clusters. That saves hand cleanup and keeps the rigid-body count manageable.

## Ground cracks

<VideoPlayer
  src="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/wolverine-houdini-vector-displacement.mp4"
  poster="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/poster-vector-displacement.webp"
  caption="COP bake for crack displacement and normals."

A heightmap can only push along the surface normal. For cracks with lateral tearing, I baked 3D vector displacement and tangent normals in Houdini COPs.

## Volume slices

<VideoPlayer
  src="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/wolverine-houdini-volume-texture.mp4"
  poster="/assets/blog/houdini-wolverine-destructibles/poster-volume-texture.webp"
  caption="Slicing a smoke volume into a texture sheet."
/>

The `slice_vdb` setup normalizes density and temperature, cuts the volume into 50 Z-slices, and packs them into a 4096 × 4096 atlas for a runtime raymarch shader. It trades a live fluid simulation for texture data that is easier to budget.
